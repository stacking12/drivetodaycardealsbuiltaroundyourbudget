Common Questions

5 FAQs

What is Buy Here Pay Here financing?

Buy Here Pay Here (BHPH) means the dealership finances the vehicle directly instead of sending you to a bank or credit union. You buy the car here and make your payments here, which is why approval and terms are decided on-site rather than by an outside lender.

Do I need good credit to get approved?

No. All credit is accepted, including no credit, bad credit, or past repossessions. Our in-house financing looks at your current ability to make payments, not just a credit score.

What documents do I need to buy a car?

Typically you'll need a valid driver's license, proof of income, and proof of residence. Bring these with you and our team can walk you through anything else needed to move your application forward quickly.

How fast can I drive away in a car?

Many qualified buyers are approved and driving the same day thanks to our instant, on-site approval process — that's the idea behind our Drive Today Car Deals.

Do you offer bilingual or Spanish-speaking service?

Yes. Our Spanish-speaking staff can guide you through the entire buying and financing process in Spanish, from your first visit to signing your paperwork.
